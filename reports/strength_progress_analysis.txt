Coaching Notes
-------------
With 2 strength sessions per week, the goal is not maximum volume. It is high-quality progression.
Focus on:
- one strong top set per target movement or close variation
- enough calories to support training quality
- high protein consistency
- low junk fatigue outside the gym
- steady weekly progression, not dramatic jumps
