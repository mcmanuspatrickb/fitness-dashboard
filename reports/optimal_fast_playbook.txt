Interpretation
--------------
This playbook is based on long fasts only.
Blocks with clear fat regain by day 14 are excluded from the reference set when that rebound data exists.
Use this as your practical template:
- controlled prep calories
- adequate protein before the fast
- moderate carbs before the fast
- controlled recovery calories
- high protein during recovery
- maintain steps during recovery
- treat the refeed as part of the intervention, not the end of it
