Time Series Data Export

The Time Series export provides a detailed timeline of your tracked activity.

Files Included:
----------

nutrition_log.csv                     - Contains all data for this type.

Each entry has the following values:

    start time                        - Start date and time of the nutrition log.
    end time                          - End date and time of the nutrition log.
    brand name                        - The brand of the food consumed.
    food name                         - The name of the food consumed.
    meal type                         - The type of meal (e.g., BREAKFAST, LUNCH, SNACK).
    nutrients                         - The nutrients in the food consumed.
    data source                       - The origin or source of this data.
